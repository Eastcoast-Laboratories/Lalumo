Help & Activity Guide

🔊 High or Low?

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: click on the mule to hear a low note or the mouse to hear a high note. Click the Bird to start the chellenge, where you hear a tone, then click the mouse for "High" or the mule for "Low" to choose which kind of tone it was.

🎵 Match Sounds

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: click the pattern buttons (up rocket, down slide, waves, frog) to hear different melody patterns. Click the bird to start the challenge, where you hear a melody and must click the pattern that matches what you heard.

✏️ Draw Melody

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can freely draw inside the sandpit and listen to the melody your painting results in. Click the Mage to start the challenge, then draw the melody's shape on the canvas. The line should go up when the melody goes higher and down when it goes lower. Click on the note on the top to hear the melody again. click the unicorn to create a new melody.

👂 Does It Sound Right?

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: click the members of the band to hear different melodies with differing iinstruments. Click the play button at the bottom to start the challenge where you listen to a melody and click the left happy animal if it sounds correct or the right sad animal if it has a few silly notes.

🧠 Memory Game

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: try the piano keys. Click the bird to start the memory challenge where you must repeat increasingly long sequences on the piano.

Chords Chapter

🦇 One or Many

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: click the deer to the left to hear one note or the bats on the right to hear many sounds. Click the owl to start the challenge, where you listen to sounds and decide if you hear one sound or many sounds playing together.

🏞️ Stable or Unstable

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: try the "Stable" left side of the forest to hear stable chords and the "Unstable" right side to hear unstable chords. Click the owl to start the challenge, where you listen to chords and decide if they sound stable (resolved) or unstable (tense).

🎭 Chord Characters

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: click different character buttons to hear different chords (major, minor, diminished, augmented). Click the owl to start the challenge, where you listen to chords and match them to their character personalities.

🌈 Magical Forest Chords

You start in Free-Play mode, where you can explore the activity: try the scattered elements in the forest to hear different chord progressions. Click the flower in the middle to start the challenge, where you listen to chord progressions and match them to their correct elements in the magical forest.
